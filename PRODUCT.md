# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UCC (Universidad Cooperativa de Colombia, Campus Pasto) students and professors exploring the platform independently on their own devices during a sustainability presentation. Secondary: any academic or corporate audience encountering the demo at future events.

## Product Purpose

A self-contained demonstration of how a software company (Ceiba Software) applies circular economy principles to its IT asset management. Built for a university sustainability course in Pasto, Nariño, Colombia, it lets viewers explore realistic seed data across five operational modules without guidance. It is a presentation demo, not a production system.

## Positioning

Bridges corporate IT operations with academic environmental science. Where typical sustainability demos show slides or static reports, CeibaCircular is a working interactive simulation that covers the full IT asset lifecycle — from procurement through reuse, repair, and responsible RAEE disposal — with quantified environmental impact at every step.

## Operating Context

Viewers open the app in a browser on personal devices (laptops, tablets, phones) during or after a live university session. No login, no setup — the app seeds itself on first run. Presenters may also project it. The environment is a university classroom or auditorium; connectivity may vary. All five modules are reachable from a persistent sidebar and work independently of each other.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five modules, each a standalone interactive view:

1. **Dashboard Ejecutivo** — KPIs (RAEE avoided, CO2 mitigated, water saved, devices reconditioned), Recharts bar and pie charts, biocentric compensation widget referencing páramos de Nariño.
2. **Inventario Circular** — Filterable table of 14 IT assets with eco-labeling (EPEAT Gold, Energy Star, TCO Certified), device registration form, and digital mineral passport (Li, Co, Au, Cu, REE per device type).
3. **Ciclo de Vida & R's** — Five-column Kanban board (En Uso → Mantenimiento → Reparación → Simbiosis UCC → Baja RAEE), R-strategy actions, NIST 800-88 secure erase checklist, RAEE disposal certificates.
4. **Calculadora GreenOps** — Interactive cloud-infrastructure carbon simulator with sliders and toggles, real-time kWh/CO2/USD/trees calculations, comparison charts.
5. **Simbiosis Académica** — Equipment donation portal linking Ceiba assets to 5 educational institutions in Nariño, with UCC student assignments, delivery timelines, and formal digital certificates.

Constraints: JSON file store (no external database); all data is simulated seed; Spanish-only UI; no authentication.

## Brand Commitments

No official brand guidelines from Ceiba Software or UCC apply. The current emerald-green and deep-blue eco-tech palette is a freely chosen design decision, not a brand mandate.

## Evidence on Hand

All seed data is fictional and illustrative. The 14 IT assets, 5 educational institutions, environmental metrics, certificates, and assignments are realistic but simulated. No real customer testimonials, case studies, press, or measured metrics exist. Future work must not present seed data as factual evidence.

## Product Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — every environmental concept (RAEE, R strategies, carbon cycle, biocentrism) is demonstrated through interactive data, not static text.
2. **Self-service clarity** — a viewer with no guide should understand what each module does and why it matters within seconds of arriving.
3. **Academic rigor with practical grounding** — concepts are scientifically accurate and tied to real-world corporate IT operations, not abstract or oversimplified.
4. **Regional anchoring** — the demo is rooted in Nariño (Pasto, Ipiales, Cumbal), its institutions, and its ecosystems (páramos, Galeras), making the environmental impact locally tangible.
5. **Honest data** — simulated data is clearly illustrative; no fabricated claims of real-world impact.
